# Litmus7: Company Brief

## Overview
Litmus7 Systems Consulting Inc. is a bootstrapped B2B retail technology engineering and AI consulting firm founded in 2009 by Venu Gopalakrishnan. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, the company employs 1,100 to 1,600+ staff globally, with major operations in India (Kochi, Trivandrum) and presence in Canada, Australia, UAE, and the UK. It serves enterprise retailers and CPG brands across grocery, fashion, beauty (e.g., Sephora), and general merchandise.

## Products and services
* **Services:** Retail Systems Engineering (digital commerce platforms, OMS, cloud microservices), Retail Consulting & Strategic Advisory, and Triode (AI-enhanced UX design and behavioral analytics).
* **Accelerators & Products:** Advait Platform (proprietary Neural Intelligence platform for pricing, replenishment, and fulfillment automation), Microservices Accelerator (MSA), L7 CT B2B Suite, and Salesforce/SAP/Oracle adapters.

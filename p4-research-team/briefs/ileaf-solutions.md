# iLeaf Solutions: Company Brief

## Overview
iLeaf Solutions (iLeaf Solutions Pvt. Ltd.) is a B2B IT services and custom software engineering consultancy founded in 2011. Headquartered in Kochi, India, with international offices in Texas (US) and Stockton-on-Tees (UK), the company employs 80+ engineers (50–200 total employees) in a pod-based structure. Executive leadership includes Co-founders Jaison Joseph (CEO) and Lovegin John (CTO). iLeaf serves mid-market and enterprise clients across North America, the UK, EU, Middle East, and Oceania in sectors such as healthcare, fintech, insurance, and real estate. Internal AI tool usage and internal budget details are Not found.

## Products and services
iLeaf offers Agentic AI engineering, legacy system modernization, custom product engineering, cloud infrastructure, data engineering, and automated QA evaluation. The company has delivered and maintained over 500 software platforms. Key proprietary offerings include:
- **iLeaf.ai:** An internal AI framework offering grounded Graph-RAG retrieval, Model Context Protocol (MCP) skill layers, runtime governance, and audit logging.
- **Adaptive AI:** A solution model to incrementally transform legacy software into agentic platforms.

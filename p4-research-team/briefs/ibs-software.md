# IBS Software: Company Brief

## Overview
IBS Software Private Limited is a Singapore-headquartered (operational HQ: Thiruvananthapuram, India) B2B SaaS provider specializing in travel technology. Founded in 1997 by Valayil Korath Mathews and led since June 2024 by CEO Somit Goyal, the company operates as a private enterprise backed by parent company Apax Partners, LLP, with prior investors including Blackstone and General Atlantic. IBS serves airlines, airports, cargo operators, hotels, cruise lines, tour operators, and energy companies across 17 offices in 10+ countries. The company employs approximately 6,000–7,800 people globally, with workforce growth of about 15% year over year. Cumulative funding stands at roughly $673.5 million USD, and its most recent secondary round raised $450 million in May 2023. Estimated revenue for FY ending March 2026 exceeds ₹1,000 crore (~$167 million), with a valuation of approximately ₹12,000 crore (~$1.4–1.5 billion). Major customers include Delta Air Lines/Delta Cargo, American Airlines Cargo, Lufthansa Cargo, OYO Hotels & Homes, Flair Airlines, Fuji Dream Airlines, STARFLYER, and Pan American World Airways (re-launching).

## Products and services
IBS offers cloud-native, AI-first platforms across multiple verticals. Its aviation hub includes iRetail (an AI-native retailing platform co-engineered with AWS supporting the full Offer-Order-Settle-Deliver value chain with self-curating product catalogs and real-time personalized offers), iFlight NEO (a cloud-native flight, crew, and disruption management platform using predictive analytics), iCargo (an air and ocean cargo management platform that processes approximately 80% of cargo movements in Japan and Australia), iLoyal for Aviation (a cloud-native loyalty program enabling real-time personalization), iFly (a passenger services platform with native NDC capabilities), and iLogistics (an AI-driven logistics platform). The hospitality hub features iStay, an agentic-ready hotel commerce platform unifying CRS, PMS, RMS, booking engine, loyalty, and distribution, alongside iLoyal for Hospitality. A comprehensive digital platform supports tour operators and cruise lines covering onshore, online, and onboard operations. IBS also provides airport operations management systems through its Moveo Systems acquisition, digital-twin-based upstream energy solutions via iLogistics AI for Upstream, and consulting/digital transformation services through its CDx business unit. The company has made several acquisitions, notably Above Property (January 2024) and AD OPT (May 2019), to expand its hospitality and North American airline capabilities.

## Likely AI needs
IBS's strategic priorities center on accelerating AI integration from proof-of-concept to production across all platforms, converging previously siloed reservations and loyalty systems for real-time personalization, and migrating customers from legacy monolithic architectures to modern modular SaaS. Specific AI needs include: (1) scaling AI-driven offer personalization and dynamic pricing optimization for iRetail, building on the ongoing AWS co-engineering collaboration and the November 2026 PROS partnership combining Kairos Offer Management with iRetail Order Management; (2) enhancing predictive disruption management and operational analytics within iFlight and iLogistics, where digital twin technology is already deployed for energy supply chains; (3) expanding real-time loyalty personalization and member engagement across iLoyal for Aviation and Hospitality, as demonstrated by deployments for STARFLYER and Pan American; and (4) addressing legacy data migration and integration challenges associated with helping customers move off outdated systems, such as Delta Cargo's transition from a 30-year-old legacy platform. CEO Somit Goyal has distinguished between AI "adopters" investing in data foundations and cross-functional teams versus "hesitators" attempting to solve modern problems with 2006-era architectures.

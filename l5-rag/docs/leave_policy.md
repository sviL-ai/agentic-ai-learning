# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per year.
Leave must be requested at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal.
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.

## Sick Leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required if sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days.
Sick leave cannot be carried over.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers get 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers get 4 weeks. Leave can be taken any time within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
